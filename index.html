About RAAZE

Some things aren't meant to be announced. They're meant to be discovered.

Origin

Why RAAZE exists

Most fragrances say everything the moment you walk in. We wanted one that holds something back — that reveals itself the way a secret does, to whoever stays close enough to notice.

Craft — 01

The Notes

Kashmiri saffron and rose open first. Given a few minutes on skin, they step aside for sandalwood, amber and a trace of vanilla underneath.

Craft — 02

The Materials

Aged sandalwood and resin, rested in small batches. Every material earns its place by how it wears through the day, not how it smells at the counter.
